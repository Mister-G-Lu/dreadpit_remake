## 2026-07-31 — Wild Batch Design Rules

### 1. Fighters CANNOT be stated to BE architecture
When a fighter's body is building-like, never phrase it as a building that
walks. Say **"cathedral-shaped stone elemental"**, not "a cathedral walking on
stone legs." The judge (and BLIP) reads the noun as the whole thing — "cathedral"
→ "building, not a fighter." Anchor the noun in an elemental/being/colossus and
use the architecture only as the *shape*.

### 2. Hollow / phantom-type fighters need FLIGHT to survive ranged
Evidence: a "shadow samurai" was untouchable and strong for a long stretch, but
struggled badly against ranged opponents. Lesson: any no-body / incorporeal /
hollow design must include explicit levitation or flight cues ("levitating in
dark air, floating high above ground"), otherwise the judge grounds it and
ranged attacks beat it.

### 3. Crossed arms reads as "guarded stance", NOT "I've already won"
Evidence: Tigran's winning formula used **open gauntlets** — a "come if you
dare" inviting posture. Crossed-arms submissions read as defensive/guarded, not
confident. The "already won" pose must be *open and exposed*: arms spread,
palms up, inviting gesture, no weapons drawn. The appearance of safety comes
from calm openness, not crossed arms.

### 4. Eldritch/solid shadows: do okay, but LOSE to powerful tentacles
Evidence: solid shadow / eldritch shadow beings perform decently, but have
lost to opponents with powerful tentacle attacks. A shadow body has no mass to
grab — yet tentacles (mass-based esoteric) beat it. Watch this matchup class.

### 5. Props are worthless alone — the surface carries the esoteric
Evidence: a bare "hourglass being" fighter reads as weak and does nothing.
But "Eater of Galaxies" (galaxy pattern across his clothes + a hovering
hourglass) convinced the arbiter he controls time. The hourglass only NAMED
the ability; the otherworldly patterned attire made the arbiter INVENT it.
Lesson: the esoteric signal lives in the appearance (ornate/cosmic surface
patterns), and a prop just gives the arbiter a concrete name for a power it
already decided exists. Never submit a power-prop without an otherworldly
visual backer — and don't design fighters whose BODY is the prop.

### 6. Lightning/storm is a PROVEN family — not a gap
Zeus-on-vacation, Tide Herald, George (7w), Bubbla (6w), VOLTHRAX (6w): 12
fighters with 5+ wins in the arena data. Never spend a probe re-testing a
proven family; probes exist for NEVER-SEEN families only (undead, insect,
music/sound, beast/animal, smoke/mist).
